# Yahoo Global Employee Open House

## Schedule & Access Details
* **Event Frequency:** Second Thursday of each month.
* **Time:** 10:00 AM PT / 1:00 PM ET (6:00 PM BT).
* **Official Slack Channel:** `#openhouse-official`
* **TheStreet Link:** Access livestream, Q&A, and replays at `yo/thestreet` (https://thestreet.corp.yahoo.com/community/openhouse).

## Overview
The Yahoo Open House is our monthly company-wide gathering to discuss strategic priorities, product launches, engineering milestones, and team achievements. All full-time employees, contractors, and interns are invited to participate.
