# Sunnyvale HQ Parking, Commuter Benefits, and EV Charging

## Parking Guidelines & Rules
* **Parking Permit Exemption:** Parking permits are NOT required for open surface lots and garages (Garage A & B). Both employees and visitors may park without permits in all open surface lots and garages (Garage A and Garage B).
* **EV Charging Limit:** EV charging stations have a strict 4-hour maximum charging limit and operate on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Pre-Tax Commuter Transit Benefits
* **Active Transit Subsidy:** Pre-tax transit pass deductions (Clipper, Caltrain) are indexed to the active IRS limit of $315 per month.
* **Historical Note:** The pre-tax transit subsidy was previously capped at $280 per month under the 2022 guidelines and has since been updated.
