# Work@Yahoo Global Remote & Travel Policy

## Policy Framework & Thresholds
* **14-Day Disclosure Rule:** The Work@Yahoo policy requires disclosing work outside your resident location if working **14 or more cumulative days** (14+ days) in a calendar year via a mandatory **Workday attestation**.
* **Active Policy:** Remote work and international travel are governed by the permanent Work@Yahoo Global Remote policy.
* **Archived COVID Rules:** The temporary 2020 emergency COVID remote work policy is officially archived and superseded.
* **Mandatory Disclosure:** Employees traveling or working remotely outside their home country or resident state must submit a Work From Abroad Attestation in Workday prior to departure.
