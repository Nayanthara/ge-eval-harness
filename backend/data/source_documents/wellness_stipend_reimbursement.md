# Yahoo Wellness Stipend Policy

## Monthly Allowance & Eligible Expenses
Eligible full-time employees receive a reimbursement of up to **$150 per month** for health, gym memberships, fitness classes, athletic shoes, and wellness expenses.

## Rollover Policy (Use-It-Or-Lose-It)
* **Zero Rollover:** Unused balances from the $150 monthly limit do NOT roll over to subsequent months. The benefit is strictly use-it-or-lose-it.
* **Historical Replacement:** This $150 monthly reimbursement fully replaced the legacy 2021 policy of a $50 quarterly partner gym discount.
