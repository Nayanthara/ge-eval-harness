# Contoso Hybrid & Remote Work Policy (2026 Edition)
**Document ID**: HR-POL-302
**Version**: 3.2 (Approved August 15, 2026)
**Owner**: Sarah Jenkins, Director of HR & People Operations

## Policy Summary
Regular full-time employees are eligible for hybrid work requiring a minimum of two designated in-office collaborative days per week (Tuesdays and Thursdays), with up to three remote days upon direct manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.
